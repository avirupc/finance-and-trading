## Where to invest?

1. Fixed Income Investments
2. Equity
3. Real Estate
4. Commodities (precious metals)

## Fixed Income Investments
Where the principal amount is perceived to be safe.

Exxample:<br>
1. Banks' fixed deposits (varies between 5-6%)
2. Bonds issued by Govt. of India
3. Bonds issued by govt. related agencies (HUDCO, GAIL, ...)
4. Bonds issued by corporates (Tata, Adani, Reliance, ...) - May be risky! 

## Equity
Buying shares of publicly listed companies.

Indian equities generated upwards 12% CAGR - can go upto 20%.

## Real Estate

Real Estate Investment involves transacting (buying and selling) commercial and non-commercial land. <br>Typical examples include transacting in vacant plots, apartments, and commercial buildings.

There are two income sources from real estate investments: 
1. Rental income - typically varies between 2-3% 
2. Capital appreciation of the investment amount - prices is in select pockets and is not uniform.

Cons:
- The transaction procedure can be quite complex involving legal verification of documents. 
- The cash outlay in real estate investment is usually quite large. - There is no official metric to measure the returns.

## Commodities (precious metals)

Buying precious metals like gold and silver.

Gold and silver, over the long term, have appreciated. Investments in these metals have yielded a CAGR return of approximately 5-8% over the last 20 years. 

Several ways to invest:
- Invest in jewelleries
- Exchange Traded Funds (EFTs)
- [Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)](https://tradingqna.com/t/what-are-sovereign-gold-bonds/6372/2)


---
### A typical suggested split of savings:

equity - 60% <br>
precious metals - 20% <br>
fixed income - 20%

## Notes
- Higher the risk, higher the return.
- Investment in fixed income is less risky but may cause loss compared to inflation.
- Equity usually beats inflation over a long period - usually 14-15%.
- Real estate cannot be done in smaller amount. Liquidity is another issue - you cannot buy or sell whenever you want.
- Gold and silver are safer - but return has not been very good.
