## Market Intermediaries

- Brokers/Stock brokers
- Depositories and Depository Participants
- Banks
- NSE clearing Limited and ICCL

## Depository and Depository Participants

A depository is a financial intermediary that offers the DEMAT account service. <br> Your broker's trading account and the DEMAT account from the depository are interlinked. <br>
In India, there are only two depositories:
- **NSDL** or National Securities Depository Limited
- **CDSL** or Central Depository Services (India) Limited

To open a demat account, you must speak to a Depository Participant (DP). A DP helps you set up your DEMAT account with a Depository. A DP acts as an intermediary between you and the Depository. Even the DP is governed by the regulations laid out by the SEBI.

Example: Zerodha is a depositary participant of Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL).

## Banks

Banks help facilitate the fund transfer from your bank account to your trading account. Both the trading account and bank account are linked. Brokers link these accounts after verifying your bank account. 

You can link multiple bank accounts to your trading through which you can transfer funds and trade. Irrespective of how many bank accounts you choose to link with your trading account, funds can be withdrawn to **only one bank account**.<br> The account you choose to withdraw funds (from your trading account) is called the **Primary account**. You can add funds to all the bank accounts, but withdrawals are only processed to the primary bank account.

Note:<br>
3 accounts are needed in total - <br>
1. trading account by broker
2. DEMAT account offered by depository participant
3. bank account offered by the bank

## NSE Clearing Limited and ICCL

1. NSE Clearing Limited - Owned by NSE
2. Indian Clearing Corporation (ICCL) -  Owned by BSE

These control the transactions.

If I am buying some shares of XYZ company, someone is selling it. If I pay x amount for it, I will be debited x rs. from my trading account and the seller must be credited x rs.

The clearing corporation -
1. identifies the buyer and seller and matches the debit and credit process
2. ensures no defaults - after selling the shares, the seller should not be able to back out, thereby defaulting in his transaction
