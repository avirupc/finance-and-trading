> #### 📌 Terminology
> - **IPO** — Initial Public Offer
> - **Promoter** — The entrepreneur who starts the business with his/her idea
> - **Angel Investors** — The people who invest in someone else's business in the pre-revenue stage are called Angel Investors
> - **Seed Fund** — The initial amount of money the business gets to kickstart the operations
> - **Venture Capitalist** — The investor who typically invests in an early stage of business
> - **Series A Funding** —  The money that the business gets at an early stage
> - **Internal Accruals** — Funding through company profits
> - **CAPEX** — Capital expenditure, the expenditure to improve overall business
> - **Series B Funding** — After few initial years, the company may approach another VC and raise another round of VC funding by allotting shares. This is called series B funding (and so on..).
> - **Debt** — Money loaned from the bank(s) for the business
> - **PE Investor** — Private Equity Investor (more in detail below)

### IPO Journey - A Brief Flow   

```text
Business Idea
    ↓
Seed Funding / Angel Investors
    ↓
Series A — Venture Capital
    ↓
Series B — More VC Funding + Debt
    ↓
Series C — Private Equity
    ↓
Business Matures & Expands
    ↓
Large Capital Requirement
    ↓
Company Considers Funding Options
    ├── Internal Accruals
    ├── More PE Funding
    ├── Bank Debt
    ├── Bonds
    └── IPO 
            ↘
            Shares Offered to the General Public
                ↓
            Public Investors Subscribe
                ↓
            Company Becomes Publicly Listed

```

### VC vs PE:
- **Stage:** VC invests in relatively early-stage businesses, while PE typically invests in more mature businesses.
- **Investment Size:** VCs generally write smaller cheques; PE firms invest much larger amounts.
- **Risk:** VC investments carry higher risk because the business is still developing. PE takes comparatively lower risk.
- **Purpose:** VC funding is often used for early expansion and growth, while PE commonly funds large CAPEX requirements and further expansion.
- **Management Involvement:** PE investors typically take a board seat and place people in the company to oversee its direction.
- **Funding Stage:** VC is associated with rounds such as **Series A/B**, whereas PE generally comes in at a later stage, such as **Series C**.
