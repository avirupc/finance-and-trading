# Zerodha Varsity Notes

This folder contains my personal notes and learnings from the different modules of [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity/).

As someone completely new to finance, trading, and the stock market, I found Varsity to be a good starting point for building a foundation and becoming familiar with the terminology, concepts, and jargon used in the field.

These notes reflect my own understanding of the material and are meant to document my learning journey. They are not intended to reproduce the original content.
