# Zerodha Varsity

[Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity/) is a free and comprehensive resource for learning about financial markets, investing, and trading.

It is particularly useful for beginners, as the material is structured into modules that gradually introduce concepts ranging from stock markets and technical analysis to derivatives, risk management, and trading systems.

## Resources

- 📚 [Varsity Modules](https://zerodha.com/varsity/modules/) — Complete collection of learning modules. The modules can also be downloaded as PDFs for offline reading.
- 🎥 [Varsity Video Modules](https://zerodha.com/varsity/video-modules/) — Video versions of selected Varsity modules.
- ▶️ [Zerodha Varsity YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@varsitybyzerodha) — Videos and playlists covering various finance, investing, and trading topics.
